LifeOS

Your All-in-One Life Management Companion

Android App No Account Needed 100% Local Storage MIT Licensed

Features

Home Dashboard

Mission control with stats grid, exam countdown, pending tasks, and quick links. Drag-to-reorder sections and persistent preferences.

University & Exams

Full exam tracking with credits, grades, progress bars, and grade simulator. Supports Italian CFU system with 18-30L scale.

Tasks & Habits

Unified task and habit manager with Today, Upcoming, Habits, and No Date sections. Recurring habits with streak tracking.

Finances

Income and expense tracking with categories, date picker, and visual spending breakdown. 6-month balance chart.

Statistics

Analytics dashboard with task completion, grade trends, graduation percentage, spending breakdown, and habit leaderboards.

Groceries

Category-based checklist with supermarket, pharmacy, home, and other tags. Filter by to buy or completed.

Notes & Links

Notes with tag autocomplete and chip system. Save links with names and emojis, starring up to 6 for quick access on home.
